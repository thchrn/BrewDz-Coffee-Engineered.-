BrewD

BrewD is a ground-coffee company built on process control. We roast, crush and screen our beans in a closed loop, so oversize particles are recycled back to the crusher until every cup gets the same consistent grind. Try our grind simulator below.

The problem we’re solving

Great coffee is a particle-size problem.

We want a rich, aromatic, earthy-dark, delicious cup, every time, from beans we can trace back to the farm.
⚙

The engineering problem

A grinder gives a wide spread of sizes. Coarse particles under-extract and taste thin or sour, while too many fines over-extract and turn bitter. Heat from over-grinding can also drive off aroma.

🎯

Our solution

We narrow the product PSD. A closed loop of crusher and screen sends oversize particles back for another pass and removes the fines, so the product sits in a tight size window.

🌱

Our promise

Good-quality beans straight from farms to your cup. Nothing is lost in the process and nothing artificial is added: the flavour you taste is the bean’s own.
